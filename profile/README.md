# LensRoll

## Saved it. Find it again.

Recipes, travel ideas, receipts, and that thing you were definitely going to remember. LensRoll turns your screenshots into a searchable library on iPhone and Android.

Text recognition runs on your device. Search the words in your screenshots, organize the keepers, and leave the originals in your photo library.

Start with up to 50 screenshots for free. LensRoll Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited screenshots and favorites. AI services and credits are not included.
